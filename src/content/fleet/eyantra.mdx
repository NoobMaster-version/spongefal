## Mission

e-Yantra (IIT Bombay's national robotics competition) set the task: a drone
that navigates a warehouse autonomously — detect and decode ArUco markers for
localization, plan paths between racks, pick and place packages, and do all of
it inside strict time limits.

## Build

- **Flight control:** PID loops tuned for stable hover and precise movement —
  the difference between a drone that drifts through a marker gate and one
  that stops on it
- **Localization:** OpenCV ArUco detection and decoding; marker positions
  triangulated into the drone's position estimate
- **Navigation:** path planning through the warehouse layout, integrated with
  the perception pipeline in real time

## Debrief

Two lessons that shaped what came after: PID tuning on a drone punishes
sloppiness instantly (a badly tuned axis is airborne, not parked), and
vision-only localization is fragile — markers occlude, lighting shifts. That
second frustration is exactly what made UWB localization at KDAIL so
interesting a year later.
