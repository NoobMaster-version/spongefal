## Mission

Build a four-legged robot that keeps walking when the floor stops being flat.
Kurma — named for the turtle avatar in Hindu mythology that carries the world —
borrows its locomotion from the animal too: slow, statically stable, hard to
tip over.

## Build

- Servo-driven legs coordinated by a Raspberry Pi
- Gait sequencing designed for **static stability**: three feet planted at all
  times, so the robot never has to catch itself
- Biomimetic gait study — actual turtle locomotion informed the leg timing
- Structural design and power management for repeated field runs

## Telemetry

Honest status: **a working prototype.** It entered the international Mass
Robotics challenge and didn't go far in the competition — but the gait held on
terrain that stops most wheeled student robots cold.

## Debrief

Legged robots collapse the boundary between mechanical design and control —
the gait *is* the structure moving. Kurma taught me to design the two together
instead of bolting a controller onto a finished frame.
