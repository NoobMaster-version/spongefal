MARIO is a small robot arm that exists twice: once in simulation, once as
real hardware running micro-ROS on an ESP32. It's what SRA uses to teach
ROS2 workshops.

The simulation was stuck on Gazebo Classic, which hit end-of-life. So I
ported the whole thing to Gazebo Fortress — model, plugins, launch files —
wrote a new teleop that's easier for beginners, and cleaned up the
ESP32 ↔ micro-ROS pipeline so the same commands drive both the sim and the
real arm.
