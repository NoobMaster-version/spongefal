NishCorp is a startup building a wheelchair that can climb stairs. When I
joined, it existed as a CAD model. My job was to make it exist in physics.

I converted the CAD to URDF, spawned it in Gazebo, applied differential-drive
kinematics, added an IMU plugin, and wrote teleop so we could actually drive
it around in sim and study the motion and steering. Then we crossed over to
the real world: 3D-printed parts, assembly, testing — a half-scale prototype
that could sit and stand.
