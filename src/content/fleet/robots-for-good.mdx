Three small builds, each made because something needed it.

## the cat feeder

Our robotics club has a cat. She doesn't care about semester breaks, so I
built her a feeder: a servo turns the dispenser on a timer, food drops down a
3D-printed chute, cat eats even when the lab is empty.

## the river boat

For my environmental studies course. A remote-controlled boat with a scoop at
the front and a basket inside: drive it around, it collects floating trash,
bring it back, empty it. Waterproofing electronics on a student budget was
the whole adventure.

## the coin sorter

24-hour hackathon. A camera looks at a coin, a small CNN on a Raspberry Pi
figures out if it's ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 or ₹10, servo gates route it into the right
box. Trained with transfer learning because there was no time for anything
else. Got above 95% on the ₹5 and ₹10 coins.
