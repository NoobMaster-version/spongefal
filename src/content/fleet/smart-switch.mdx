Normal wall switches, but you can also flip them from your phone, from
anywhere. The rule I set myself: the physical switches must keep working no
matter what — wifi down, cloud down, app deleted, doesn't matter.

I built every layer. Designed the box in CAD, soldered a perf board with
relays and optocouplers (there's 220 V on one side of that board and a 3.3 V
ESP32 on the other — the optocouplers are not optional), wrote the firmware
on ESP-IDF, set up an MQTT broker with TLS, and made a Flutter app.

Some details I'm fond of:

- wifi setup happens over BLE — the app sends the ssid and password, nothing
  is hardcoded
- state survives power cuts (NVS), and the board sends a small heartbeat
  every 5 seconds so the app always knows the truth
- the wall switch, the firmware, the cloud and the app all have to agree on
  whether a light is on. keeping four copies of the truth in sync was the
  hardest part of the whole project — harder than the mains voltage
- the app was honestly vibe-coded. the firmware was not.
