Voice Video Manipulator. You say what you want, a camera finds it, an arm
grabs it. All in Gazebo, with an Open Manipulator X.

The pipeline: speech recognition turns your words into a target ("red ball"),
object detection finds it in the camera frame, some geometry turns the 2D
pixel into a 3D position, and inverse kinematics moves the arm there.
